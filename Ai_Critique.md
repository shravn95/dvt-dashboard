# AI as Critic: Daily Activity Spike Visualization

## 1. Visualization Evaluated
**Chart:** Daily Activity Spike  
**Dataset:** Stanford SNAP CollegeMsg temporal message network  
**Highlighted observation:** May 27, 2004, recorded 2,677 messages.

## 2. AI Criticism
The chart highlights a large activity spike, but the visualization alone cannot explain why the spike occurred. Comparing it with the average of days that had activity may also exaggerate the difference if zero-message calendar days are excluded.

## 3. Decision
**Partially Accepted**

## 4. Justification
The spike is useful for identifying an unusual activity date, but the chart does not establish its cause. The average should be clearly defined, and the finding should be presented as a descriptive observation rather than evidence of a specific event or cause.

## 5. Improvement
Clearly state how the daily average is calculated. If the objective is to compare activity across the full calendar period, include zero-message days in the baseline. Retain the spike visualization while avoiding unsupported conclusions about its cause.
